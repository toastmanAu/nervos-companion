# Privacy Policy for CKB Directory Companion App

**Effective Date:** July 24, 2026

**CKB Directory** ("we", "our", or "us") is an unofficial companion application for the Nervos Network (CKB). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through compliance with this privacy policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The CKB Directory application is designed with a privacy-first approach:

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, phone number, physical address, or contact list.
- **Local Settings & Preferences:** Your settings, favorite ecosystem apps, RPC node selections, and UI preferences are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and Room database.
- **Blockchain Data:** When you view blockchain data, perform RPC queries, or calculate DAO yields, the application connects directly to the public CKB node or custom RPC endpoint you configure. No query data is routed to our private servers.

## 2. Dynamic Remote Configuration

The app periodically fetches dynamic ecosystem listings (`apps.json`) and news feed items from official or community-curated GitHub repositories. These HTTP requests only fetch static JSON files and do not transmit tracking identifiers.
